# Wiki Guidelines
---

contributing to the wiki is alright AND encouraged with the rate that we work on, stuff may become outdated quickly so contributors that can keep pages up to day are needed......but keep these in mind

1. ***Pages should use the .mdx file format:*** this is due to making it easier to insert components (notes, inline code, etc.) in the pages. also due to the workaround one has to do rn regarding embed paths. idk why astro screws them up when building and why dev paths is differnt from build paths

2. ***Pages must contain actual, useful information for common problems:*** its not really worth having pages pure of very specific niche stuff like "how to make a change character event". please actually try to make a simple page for beginner problems/tutorials. very common problems ***regarding vanilla cne stuff (not public scripts)*** should try to go in the [troubleshooting](src/content/wiki/troubleshooting.mdx) page

3. ***Information must come from human sources:*** machines don't know any better than what humans can, really, a human-written page is much more trustable and other humans may correct you if you get something wrong. its part of common sense

4. ***Pages must not get extremely technical, unless stated by difficulty:*** anything on the basics-average range should avoid throwing advanced stuff into your brain for no reason. a learning curve must be present (it will be polished eventually)

5. ***There can be funny, but not a lot of it:*** too much salt ruins the dish. this ties in very close to **#2** where the main focus should be, well, ACTUAL information and not just jokes. stuff Can be worded informally but dont overuse it because then the article cannot be taken seriously and may be dismissed

6. ***Organize your points well:*** it would not be fun having to jump from one header to the last one to the third one to he second one to the sixth one to the first one again. i think you know where i'm going with this (this might just be common sense)
